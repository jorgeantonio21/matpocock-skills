Add the `pragmatic-programming` skill (in-progress bucket, model-invoked). A reference of tips from _The Pragmatic Programmer_ cut down to the ones a diff can be judged against, each reading what it looks like → the move, in four sections: **Bugs** (prove it, contracts, crash early, assertions, resources, shared state, coincidence), **Craft** (DRY, orthogonality, tell don't ask, method chains, globals, inheritance tax, design to test, naming), **Design** (easier to change, fortune-telling, decoupling, data over state, configuration, simplicity), and **Building** (tracer bullets, small steps, quality as a requirement). Entries that overlap a Fowler smell from `code-review`'s Standards baseline are bracketed so a review running both reports the smell once. Foundation for the `full-review`, `pair-by-commit`, and `pair-by-plan` skills that follow.
