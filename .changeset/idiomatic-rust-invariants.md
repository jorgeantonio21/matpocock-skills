`idiomatic-rust`: teach general invariant patterns and contextual Rust idioms (fork issue #8). A new `INVARIANTS.md` names three kinds of guarantee (intrinsic, aggregate, contextual admission) and eight patterns, each with the guarantee, what it prevents, when it applies, and what is enough instead. Every Rust block in it, and in `SKILL.md`, `RUNTIME.md`, and `CRATES.md`, is a verbatim excerpt of a compiled, tested, lint-clean `examples/` crate, and `evals/check.sh` fails when they drift. Two snippets changed to pass the skill's own check: `select!` arms bind `()` rather than `_`, and the drain closure ends its call with a semicolon. `SKILL.md` opens the Shape section with "State the guarantee before you pick the type" (a wrapper for an invariant, a confusable role, or behavior; the primitive otherwise), adds "Close every route" (a private field stops nothing that a derived `Deserialize` or a delegating `FromStr` builds), fixes the `const fn new` example (`then_some` is not const-callable on stable Rust; an `if` is), makes typestate and phantom types a response to a named bug, adds the outcome-carries-the-facts and shared-interpretation rules and an optional module-local `Result` alias, and says which rules are requirements, defaults, or conventions. `RUNTIME.md` decides a hot path by requirement and measurement rather than by program category, keeps the hot-path rule absolute (no lock, and no structure that locks or parks inside, whatever the alternative costs), lists the alternatives in order, and leaves the choice of mutex for the edge to the project. It also corrects the dynosaur claim (the trait object still boxes each future) and the `Send` advice (assert the future, not only the type). `CRATES.md` replaces the "Do not reach for" list with selection guidance by capability for error, builder, validation, and async-trait crates, keeps the three std replacements, states the proc-macro cost precisely (the emitted code, read with `cargo expand`), and corrects the `EnumIter` cost (a stack struct, no heap allocation).

The evaluation suite gains four scenarios, each with external semantic tests and a reference solution: a decoder library (two routes that bypass `new`, a negation at `i32::MIN`), an inference request scheduler (a policy change that must keep admitted work, a retirement whose outcome is the record), a small CLI (a stricter config version that must keep a version 1 file's meaning), and a plain crate with one defect where the right diff is tiny. `run.sh` accumulates runs (`r1`, `r2`, ...) and takes a `skill@<git-ref>` arm, so the merged skill and a revision are compared on one model, and every run records the skill commit it loaded. `score.sh` runs the external tests, reports the diff against `start/`, applies a scenario's `check-flags` to both passes, and reports `BUILD FAILED` and `INCOMPLETE` instead of zero findings when cargo fails. `analyze.py matrix` aggregates runs into a result table. Two fixtures were cleaned (`s1`'s empty file, two unplanned lint findings in `s3`'s tests). The 2026-09-04 results are preserved. The revision has not been run against a model, and `evals/README.md` lists what promotion still needs.
